## Non-negotiable visual rules learned from approval

- Spanish labels.
- Recognizable D&D paper-sheet lineage without tracing official artwork.
- Writable whitespace is functional capacity, not wasted space.
- Writing rules remain visible even when generated text is prefilled.
- Text, rules and markers must share an intentional Y-axis rhythm; generated text must not sit on rule lines.
- Fixed/calculated lookup values may use compact decorative framing.
- Player-maintained prose/list information receives practical ruled/table writing area.
- Training uses the approved single/double proficiency marker grammar consistently.
- Every Ability/skill visibly preserves its governing Attribute relationship.
- Custom Attributes/Abilities receive equivalent informational completeness.
- Extended pages must look native to the Classic family, not like generic reports/dashboards.
- Blank rows/boxes remain useful for handwriting.

## Freeze boundary

Do not change the approved Classic geometry/visual grammar merely for cleanup or stylistic preference.

A change is justified only by:

- a new owner-observed defect;
- a demonstrated data-driven overflow/readability case;
- or an explicit product requirement that the frozen proof did not exercise.

When productionizing, prefer extracting/reusing approved components rather than redrawing the family from scratch.
